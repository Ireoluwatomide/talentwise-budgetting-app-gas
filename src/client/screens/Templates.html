Apply a budget template based on your monthly income. This will create or update spending goals for each category.
What These Mean
50/30/20 Rule
50% Needs (housing, food, transport, utilities, health) · 30% Wants (entertainment) · 20% Savings & debt repayment
70/20/10 Rule
70% Living expenses (housing, food, transport, utilities) · 20% Savings · 10% Debt repayment & giving
